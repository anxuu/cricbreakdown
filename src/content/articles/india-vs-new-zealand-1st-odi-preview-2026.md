## 3. Pitch Report

Eden Park generally provides a balanced surface with opportunities for both batters and fast bowlers. The pitch can offer movement and bounce during the early overs, while batters who settle in can take advantage of the relatively short boundaries.

The ground's dimensions make it particularly attractive for aggressive stroke play, and the outfield generally rewards well-timed shots.

### Pitch Characteristics

- **Spin:** Spinners can contribute through accuracy and variations.

## 8. My POV and Match Prediction

### If India Bat First

If India negotiate the initial movement successfully and establish a strong opening partnership, they could target 270–290 runs. However, early wickets could expose the middle order and allow New Zealand to control the innings.

### If New Zealand Bat First

New Zealand could look to exploit the powerplay and build momentum through the middle overs. Daryl Mitchell and Glenn Phillips could become crucial in pushing the total towards 280–300.

### If India Bowl First

India's biggest opportunity will be taking early wickets. Their fast bowlers will need to make effective use of the new ball, while Kuldeep Yadav could become decisive once New Zealand's middle order settles.

### If New Zealand Bowl First

New Zealand could exploit early movement and put pressure on India's top order. Removing Rohit Sharma, Shubman Gill and Virat Kohli early could significantly influence the contest.

### My Final Prediction

New Zealand hold a slight advantage because of home conditions, but India have the experience and batting quality to challenge them strongly.
