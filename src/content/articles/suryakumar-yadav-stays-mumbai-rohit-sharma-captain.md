Mumbai Indians are preparing for a major reset ahead of IPL 2027. With Hardik Pandya reportedly heading to Punjab Kings, the franchise are determined to hold on to Suryakumar Yadav and are also set to bring Rohit Sharma back as captain.

In this article, we look at why MI are not willing to trade Surya, how Rohit’s return came about, and where Tilak Varma fits into the team's long-term leadership plans.

## Suryakumar Yadav Is Not Leaving Mumbai

After the Hardik Pandya trade developments, Kolkata Knight Riders and Lucknow Super Giants were reportedly interested in Suryakumar Yadav. KKR and LSG have been exploring leadership options, making an experienced player like Surya an attractive target.

But Mumbai are not keen to let him go. Cricbuzz has reported that MI sources made their position clear: Suryakumar is not available for trade, and there have been no discussions to move him. That is a strong message to any franchise hoping to sign him.

This is a sensible call. Losing Surya and Hardik in the same window would have been a major change to the core of the team. Suryakumar had a difficult IPL 2026 campaign, scoring 270 runs in 13 matches, but his quality and experience are not in doubt. Mumbai appear ready to back him to bounce back rather than let a rival team benefit.

## Rohit Sharma Set to Captain Mumbai Again

Rohit Sharma is now likely to return as Mumbai Indians captain for IPL 2027. The Indian Express reported that the franchise wanted him to take charge again and that Rohit initially suggested Tilak Varma as a long-term leadership option. The management, however, requested Rohit to take the captaincy himself, and he has agreed, according to the report.

That would bring Mumbai back to the captain who led them to five IPL titles. It also gives the franchise an experienced leader as they reshape the squad following a difficult 2026 season.

The appointment has been widely reported, but the franchise has not yet made a formal public announcement. The timing of the announcement could depend on the retention process and the team's preparations for the auction.

### What It Means

With Suryakumar staying and Rohit expected to return as captain, Mumbai are trying to keep their experienced core intact while planning for the future. Tilak Varma remains part of that long-term picture, even if he is not taking over the captaincy immediately.

If Arshdeep Singh's reported move to Mumbai is completed, he would also join Jasprit Bumrah to strengthen the pace attack. MI would then enter the auction with a clearer leadership structure and some important squad questions already addressed.

Respect to Mumbai: they are making decisive moves, but the final picture will become clear once the trades and captaincy announcement are officially confirmed.
