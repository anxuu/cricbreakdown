In this blog, we'll discuss why Mumbai Indians rejected every offer for Suryakumar Yadav and how Rohit Sharma is set to return as captain. We will also look at the Tilak Varma plan and what this means for MI going into the auction.

## Suryakumar Yadav Is Not Leaving Mumbai

Right after the Hardik deal, KKR and LSG reportedly approached Mumbai about Suryakumar Yadav. Mumbai put out an official statement: no offer, no player package, nothing will move them. Cricbuzz reports Rohit Sharma played a big part in convincing the team to keep him.

This is smart. Losing Surya and Hardik in the same window would have been a mistake. After a poor season and being dropped from the national team, Surya looks hungry to prove himself. Teams that were hoping to pick him up will have to look elsewhere.

## Rohit Sharma Set to Captain Mumbai Again

Mumbai reportedly told Rohit to take full control from next season. He replied that Tilak Varma should be the long-term captain, with Rohit overseeing things from above. Mumbai pushed back: if he is going to be involved anyway, he should hold the captaincy himself, with full freedom.

That is almost done, and the signs are already visible in the coaching department. But Mumbai are in no rush to announce it:

- It could come around the retention deadline
- It could come after the auction
- Most likely, we celebrate around December

### What It Means

With Arshdeep Singh joining Jasprit Bumrah, Suryakumar staying, and Rohit back in charge, Mumbai have fixed their problems in a single trade window. Tilak Varma stays in the picture as the long-term leader. Respect to Mumbai: sharp negotiators, again.
