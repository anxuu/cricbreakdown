The Hnry Stadium pitch report suggests that the surface could favour fast bowlers, especially during the powerplay.

## 8. My POV and Match Prediction

India enter the second ODI with a 1-0 advantage after winning the opening encounter by five wickets through the DLS method.

However, New Zealand will be eager to make a comeback, especially with home conditions working in their favour.

### If India Bat First

India will look to establish a strong opening partnership through Rohit Sharma and Shubman Gill. Virat Kohli's excellent form will provide another major advantage.

If the Indian top order negotiates the initial movement successfully, they could target 260–280 runs.

However, New Zealand's pace attack could create problems with the new ball, particularly if there is cloud cover.

### If New Zealand Bat First

New Zealand will need a much-improved batting performance after being dismissed for 185 in the opening ODI.

Will Young, Tim Seifert and Henry Nicholls will be important during the powerplay, while Daryl Mitchell and Glenn Phillips could play crucial roles in building the middle-order innings.

A strong partnership between the middle-order batters could help New Zealand push towards the 270-run mark.

### If India Bowl First

India's pace attack will once again be a major factor.

Arshdeep Singh and Anshul Kamboj will look to exploit the early conditions, while Gurnoor Brar will be another important option.

Kuldeep Yadav could become decisive during the middle overs, particularly if New Zealand's batters look to accelerate.

### If New Zealand Bowl First

New Zealand will look to exploit the early movement and put pressure on India's top order.

Kyle Jamieson, Matt Henry and Jacob Duffy could be particularly dangerous if the conditions favour swing and seam movement.

Removing Rohit Sharma, Shubman Gill and Virat Kohli early could significantly change the match's direction.

### Weather Factor between IND vs NZ 2nd ODI

Weather could become one of the biggest talking points in the IND vs NZ 2nd ODI.

The Wellington forecast indicates a possibility of light rain around match time, with overcast conditions potentially assisting fast bowlers.

Both captains will need to consider possible interruptions when making their toss and batting decisions.

### My Final Prediction

India enter the contest with greater confidence following their opening ODI victory. Their bowling attack has looked effective, while Virat Kohli's form provides stability to the batting lineup.

However, New Zealand should not be underestimated, particularly with home conditions and a strong pace attack at their disposal.

The powerplay battle, India's middle-order response to short-pitched bowling and the weather could determine the outcome.
