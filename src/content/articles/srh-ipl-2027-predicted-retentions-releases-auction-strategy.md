## SUNRISERS HYDERABAD IPL 2027 SQUAD PREDICTION: FINAL VERDICT

Sunrisers Hyderabad's predicted IPL 2027 retention strategy revolves around maintaining their explosive batting core while improving their overall squad balance.

Abhishek Sharma, Travis Head, Ishan Kishan and Heinrich Klaasen would continue to provide the franchise with a dangerous batting combination.

Pat Cummins and Nitish Kumar Reddy would offer valuable all-round options, while Kamindu Mendis could provide additional flexibility.

The retention of Jaydev Unadkat would maintain experienced Indian fast-bowling options, while Eshan Malinga could provide further pace depth.

However, the biggest talking point remains SRH's recent trade activity involving T. Natarajan, Khaleel Ahmed and Nathan Ellis. These three additions could significantly strengthen the franchise's bowling resources.

With 18 players accounted for, seven available squad positions and an estimated ₹22.20 crore auction purse, Sunrisers Hyderabad could enter the IPL 2027 auction with a clear strategy of strengthening their remaining weaknesses rather than rebuilding their entire squad.
