# India Test Squad for New Zealand Tour 2026

The BCCI has announced India's squad for the two-Test series in New Zealand in November 2026. Shubman Gill will captain the side, with KL Rahul named vice-captain. The 16-player squad includes Jasprit Bumrah, Ravindra Jadeja, Mohammed Siraj and returning fast bowler Akash Deep.

Rishabh Pant has been selected subject to fitness clearance. The series is scheduled to begin in Wellington on 19 November, with the second Test in Christchurch from 27 November.

## Key players to watch

### Shubman Gill
Gill will lead India's Test side on the tour. His top-order role will be important as India adjust to New Zealand conditions.

### Jasprit Bumrah
Bumrah returns to the Test squad and adds a major pace option for conditions that can offer movement and bounce.

### Ravindra Jadeja and Washington Sundar
The two all-rounders give India spin options as well as batting depth, while Kuldeep Yadav adds a specialist wrist-spin option.

### Akash Deep
Akash Deep is included among India's seamers, providing another pace option alongside Bumrah, Siraj, Prasidh Krishna and Anshul Kamboj.

## Is Rishabh Pant available for the New Zealand Tests?

Pant has been named in the squad, but his selection is subject to fitness clearance. His final availability depends on the required medical clearance.

## Who is India's captain and vice-captain for the Test series?

Shubman Gill is the captain and KL Rahul is the vice-captain.

## How many Tests will India play in New Zealand in 2026?

India are scheduled to play two Tests: the first in Wellington from 19 November and the second in Christchurch from 27 November.
