The **India Test squad for the New Zealand tour 2026** features Shubman Gill as captain and KL Rahul as vice-captain. The 16-player group includes established names such as Jasprit Bumrah, Ravindra Jadeja and Mohammed Siraj, along with options including Akash Deep, Anshul Kamboj and Sai Sudharsan.

Rishabh Pant has been named subject to fitness clearance. India are scheduled to play two Tests in New Zealand in November 2026.

## Key players in India's Test squad

### Shubman Gill

Gill will lead the side on the New Zealand tour. His role at the top of the order and as captain will be central to India's plans in overseas conditions.

### Jasprit Bumrah

Bumrah gives India a frontline pace option. His ability to generate movement and maintain control makes him a key bowler in conditions that can assist seam.

### Ravindra Jadeja and Washington Sundar

Jadeja and Sundar offer all-round value, combining batting depth with spin options. Kuldeep Yadav adds a specialist wrist-spinner to the squad.

### Akash Deep and the pace group

Akash Deep joins Bumrah, Siraj, Prasidh Krishna and Anshul Kamboj among the listed seam-bowling options, giving India several combinations to consider.
