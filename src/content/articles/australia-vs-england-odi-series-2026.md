International cricket's oldest rivalry returns as Australia prepare to host England for a three-match ODI series beginning on November 13, 2026. The series will be played across Perth, Adelaide and Hobart, with both teams looking to strengthen their combinations and build towards the 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup.

The ODI series will be followed by a five-match T20I series, making this an important white-ball assignment for both sides.

## Why Does Australia vs England ODI Series Matter?

With the 2027 ODI World Cup approaching, the Australia-England series offers both teams an opportunity to assess their combinations, identify areas for improvement and strengthen their preparations for the global tournament.

For Australia, the series presents an opportunity to test their squad depth, particularly in the absence of experienced fast bowlers Pat Cummins, Mitchell Starc and Josh Hazlewood, who have been rested ahead of upcoming Test commitments.

England, meanwhile, will look to make the most of their overseas assignment by testing their batting and bowling combinations against Australian conditions.

The series also provides an opportunity for both teams to evaluate their ODI combinations ahead of the World Cup. ## Key Questions Ahead of the Series

### 1. Can Joe Root and Ben Duckett Provide Consistency?

Joe Root's experience and ability to anchor innings will be crucial for England, particularly if early wickets fall. Ben Duckett's aggressive approach at the top could help England establish momentum during the powerplay. Their ability to combine stability with attacking intent will be important against Australia's bowling attack.

### 2. How Important Will Travis Head and Mitchell Marsh Be?

Travis Head's aggressive batting could give Australia a strong start, especially during the powerplay. Mitchell Marsh, meanwhile, will be important both as captain and as a key contributor with the bat. Their performances could significantly influence Australia's scoring momentum and overall batting strength.

### 3. Can Jofra Archer Make a Difference in Australian Conditions?

Jofra Archer's pace, bounce and ability to trouble batters with short-pitched deliveries could make him a major threat in Australian conditions. His effectiveness with the new ball and during crucial middle-over passages could prove important in England's attempt to challenge Australia's batting lineup.

## Conclusion

The Australia-England ODI series promises an exciting contest between two competitive sides with important preparations ahead of the 2027 ODI World Cup. With experienced players, emerging talents and several key battles to watch, the three-match series could provide valuable insights into both teams' strengths and combinations.
