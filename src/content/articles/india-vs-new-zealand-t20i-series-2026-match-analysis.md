## 1. Series Overview

The five-match T20I series between India and New Zealand was a closely contested contest that saw India establish early dominance before New Zealand fought back in the final two matches.

India won the first three matches to secure the series, demonstrating strong batting performances and the ability to chase challenging totals.

New Zealand responded by winning the fourth and fifth T20Is, finishing the series with two consecutive victories. Their improved batting performances, particularly from Glenn Phillips, provided a strong finish to the campaign.

Although New Zealand won the final two matches, India's early advantage proved decisive as they secured the series 3–2.

- **3rd T20I:** India won by 6 wickets.

- **5th T20I:** New Zealand won by 7 wickets.

**Final series score:** India 3–2 New Zealand.

## 3. Team Performance Analysis

### India: Series Winners

India's biggest strength was their ability to win the first three matches and establish an advantage that New Zealand could not overcome.

Their batting unit produced important contributions throughout the series, while their ability to chase targets helped them secure the opening three victories.

However, the final two matches exposed areas that required improvement. New Zealand's bowling restricted India in the fourth T20I, while their batting attack successfully chased 192 in the fifth.

India finished as deserved series winners, but their performances in the final two matches showed that consistency across all five games remained an area to improve.

### New Zealand: Strong Finish

New Zealand struggled to convert opportunities in the opening three matches, losing all of them.

However, their performance improved significantly in the final two games.

The 87-run victory in the fourth T20I demonstrated their batting strength, while the successful chase in the fifth highlighted their improved ability to handle pressure.

Their late comeback was particularly important because it prevented India from completing a clean sweep.

## 4. Player Performance Analysis

### Glenn Phillips: Player of the Series

Phillips was the standout batter of the series, finishing with 341 runs.

His consistent contributions, including a century in the fourth T20I and an unbeaten 65 in the fifth, helped New Zealand finish strongly.

His 341 runs established a new record for the most runs in a bilateral T20I series, surpassing Phil Salt's previous record of 331.

### Tim Seifert: Outstanding Final-Match Contribution

Seifert's 93 off 50 balls in the fifth T20I was one of the most important individual performances of the series finale. His partnership with Phillips ensured New Zealand completed the chase comfortably.

### Adam Milne: Match-Winning Bowling Performance

Milne's 4/19 in the fifth T20I was instrumental in restricting India to 191/8. His early breakthroughs placed India under pressure and helped New Zealand control the first innings.

### Ishan Kishan: Important Batting Contribution

Kishan's 61 off 32 balls in the fifth T20I helped India recover after losing two early wickets. His partnership with Shreyas Iyer was a major highlight of India's innings.
