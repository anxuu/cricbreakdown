### WHY KKR WOULD LIKE TO RETAIN MATHEESHA PATHIRANA

Matheesha Pathirana could be an important retention for KKR ahead of IPL 2027 because of his death-bowling capabilities and unique bowling action.

- **Death Bowling:** His yorkers and variations could strengthen KKR during the final overs.
- **Unique Action:** His slingy action makes him difficult for batters to line up consistently.
- **Bowling Combination:** Pathirana alongside Harshit Rana could provide KKR with a strong pace-bowling combination.
- **Specialist Role:** Retaining him would allow KKR to maintain an established death-over specialist rather than searching for a replacement at the auction.

At ₹18 crore, Pathirana represents a significant investment, but his specialist bowling role could justify his retention.
