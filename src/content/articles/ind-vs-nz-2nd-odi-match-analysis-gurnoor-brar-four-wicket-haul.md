# IND vs NZ 2nd ODI Match Analysis: Gurnoor Brar's Four-Wicket Haul Headlines India's Victory

India secured a thrilling four-run victory over New Zealand via the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method in the second ODI at Hnry Stadium, Wellington, on November 7, 2026. Gurnoor Brar led India's bowling attack with an outstanding four-wicket haul, while KL Rahul and Shreyas Iyer played important roles during a difficult chase.

New Zealand were bowled out for 134 in 41 overs. India reached 113/7 in 26 overs before rain stopped play. With India marginally ahead on the DLS par score, the visitors were awarded a four-run win and took a 2-0 lead in the five-match series.

## New Zealand Batting: Daryl Mitchell Provides the Resistance

New Zealand struggled against India's pace attack after being asked to bat first. Early wickets left the hosts under pressure, and they were reduced to 23/3 inside the first nine overs. Daryl Mitchell then held the innings together with a patient 55 off 104 balls.

Mitchell found support from Mitchell Santner, and the pair added 59 runs for the sixth wicket. Santner scored 31 from 38 deliveries, but India's bowlers kept striking at key moments. Gurnoor Brar removed Santner and continued to trouble the lower order, while Axar Patel dismissed Mitchell and finished with two wickets.

New Zealand were eventually bowled out for 134 in 41 overs, leaving India a target of 135 in a rain-affected match.

## Gurnoor Brar's Four-Wicket Haul Turns the Match

Gurnoor Brar was the standout performer with the ball, taking 4/30 from his 10 overs. His use of bounce and disciplined line made scoring difficult, and his wickets prevented New Zealand from building a late-innings surge.

Auqib Nabi and Anshul Kamboj picked up two wickets each, putting New Zealand's top and middle order under pressure. Axar Patel also claimed two important wickets, including the dismissal of Daryl Mitchell, New Zealand's top scorer.

## India's Chase: KL Rahul and Shreyas Iyer Keep India in the Contest

India's chase of 135 began with pressure at the top. Jacob Duffy removed Shubman Gill and Virat Kohli early, while Kyle Jamieson dismissed Rohit Sharma and later Shreyas Iyer. Rohit made 23 from 28 balls, and Shreyas contributed 30 from 46 deliveries.

Axar Patel could add only seven before falling to Matthew Fisher. With wickets going down, KL Rahul provided composure in the middle order. His unbeaten 26 from 38 balls, alongside Naman Dhir's 12, kept India moving towards the target.

India were 113/7 after 26 overs when rain stopped play. They still needed 22 runs on the ordinary target, but the DLS calculation placed them four runs ahead, confirming India's victory.
