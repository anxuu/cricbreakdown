The third ODI between India and New Zealand at Seddon Park, Hamilton, ended without a result after persistent rain prevented any play on November 10, 2026. Despite the delayed start and an eventual reduction to 40 overs per side, the weather continued to interrupt proceedings, forcing the umpires to abandon the match.

India continue to lead the five-match ODI series 2-0, securing an unassailable advantage and putting New Zealand under pressure heading into the final two matches.

## India vs New Zealand 3rd ODI: Rain Disrupts Hamilton Match

Rain remained the biggest talking point throughout the third ODI in Hamilton. After a significant delay, the match was initially reduced to 40 overs per side, with India captain Shubman Gill choosing to bowl first.

However, just as the ground staff were preparing the playing surface, rain returned and prevented the match from getting underway. Despite multiple inspections and efforts to resume play, the conditions did not improve sufficiently for cricket.

Eventually, the officials abandoned the contest without a single ball being bowled.

## Shubman Gill Wins Toss: India Opt to Bowl First

India captain Shubman Gill won the toss for the third consecutive ODI and elected to field, hoping to utilise the bowling-friendly conditions at Seddon Park.

India went with an unchanged playing XI, while New Zealand included Matthew Fisher in place of Blair Tickner.

Unfortunately, the toss was the only major on-field development before rain brought the match to an end.

## India vs New Zealand ODI Series 2026: India Take Unassailable 2-0 Lead

The washout ensured that India could no longer lose the five-match ODI series. Having won the opening two encounters, the visitors secured the series advantage with two matches still remaining.

New Zealand, meanwhile, will now look to finish the series strongly in front of their home supporters.

The result also brought an end to New Zealand's remarkable run of 11 consecutive home bilateral ODI series victories, dating back to 2019.

## IND vs NZ 4th ODI 2026: Focus Shifts to Mount Maunganui

With the Hamilton ODI abandoned, both teams will now travel to Mount Maunganui for the remaining two matches.

India are expected to make changes to their lineup, with Shreyas Iyer set to captain the side for the final two ODIs following Shubman Gill's departure to join the Test squad.

New Zealand will aim to bounce back and avoid a series whitewash, while India will look to maintain their dominance in the remaining fixtures.

## India vs New Zealand 3rd ODI 2026: Final Verdict

The IND vs NZ 3rd ODI in Hamilton turned into a disappointing washout, with persistent rain denying fans the opportunity to witness another competitive encounter.
