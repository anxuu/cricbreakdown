Mumbai Indians enter IPL 2027 with crucial squad decisions ahead. Their retentions, overseas combinations and Hardik Pandya's future could significantly shape their upcoming campaign.

## NEW COACHING STAFF & MANAGEMENT

Mumbai Indians are set to enter IPL 2027 under the coaching leadership of Kieron Pollard, bringing a fresh direction to the franchise. Rohit Sharma is also expected to return to captaincy, potentially creating an interesting leadership combination.

Pollard's appointment could mark a new chapter for MI as they look to rebuild their title-winning identity.

## HARDIK PANDYA TRADE: PBKS OR CSK?

Hardik Pandya's future remains MI's biggest talking point, with Punjab Kings emerging as a potential destination.

A possible exchange involving Arshdeep Singh and Vishnu Vinod could strengthen MI's bowling attack, particularly by adding a left-arm pacer alongside Jasprit Bumrah.

CSK were also previously linked with Hardik, but their interest reportedly cooled. If MI receive a valuable player in return, trading Hardik could prove beneficial.

Otherwise, Hardik could remain at Mumbai and potentially play under Rohit Sharma again in IPL 2027.

## PURSE & AUCTION BUDGET

Mumbai Indians' predicted auction purse will largely depend on Hardik Pandya's future.

| Scenario | Predicted Purse |
|---|---:|
| HARDIK PANDYA RETAINED | ₹16.45 CR |
| HARDIK PANDYA TRADED | ₹32.80 CR |

If Hardik remains at Mumbai, MI could enter the auction with approximately ₹16.45 crore. However, a trade involving Hardik could significantly increase their available purse, depending on the incoming player's contract value.

This difference could prove crucial in determining MI's auction strategy and their ability to strengthen multiple departments.

## CONTROVERSIAL RETENTIONS: BOSCH & DE KOCK

Corbin Bosch and Quinton de Kock could become two of MI's most debated retention decisions.

Bosch offers all-round capabilities, but his retention could be questioned considering MI's overseas combination and the availability of other options.

De Kock remains an experienced international wicketkeeper-batter, but retaining both him and Ryan Rickelton could create an overlap in roles.

Their experience, flexibility and familiarity with the franchise could nevertheless be reasons behind retaining them, despite questions surrounding their recent performances and squad fit.

## AUCTION STRATEGY

MI's biggest priority should be strengthening their bowling attack.

Potential auction targets should include:

- A dependable overseas fast bowler
- A quality death-overs specialist
- Additional Indian pace-bowling depth

If Arshdeep Singh arrives through a trade, MI could focus more heavily on an overseas death bowler and additional batting depth.

## FINAL PREDICTION

MI should retain their experienced core, back promising Indian youngsters and strengthen their bowling attack.

The Hardik trade could define their IPL 2027 campaign. With Pollard's coaching, a settled leadership structure and the right auction additions, Mumbai Indians could once again challenge for the title.
