The **ICC Men’s ODI World Cup 2027** will be played from **October 2 to November 21, 2027**, across **South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia**. The 14-team tournament will feature **57 matches across 12 venues**, with the final scheduled at the Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg on November 21.

India are in **Group A** alongside Australia, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Zimbabwe and Qualifier A. India will begin their campaign against **Australia on October 7 in Cape Town**, followed by the highly anticipated **India vs Pakistan match on October 10 in Johannesburg**.

## ICC ODI World Cup 2027 Schedule

The 2027 ODI World Cup begins with a three-team **Super Series in Windhoek, Namibia**, from October 2 to October 6. The winner of the Super Series will take the final Group Stage position.

The Group Stage then begins on October 7, followed by the Super 7 phase from October 25 to November 14. The semi-finals will be played on November 17 and 18, with the final on November 21.

The three lowest-ranked teams from the 2027 World Cup qualification field will compete in the Super Series. They will play a single round-robin, with the team finishing first qualifying for the Group Stage.

India will open their 2027 World Cup campaign against defending champions Australia on **October 7 in Cape Town**. The fixture is a repeat of the 2023 World Cup final.

India and Pakistan will meet on **October 10, 2027, at the Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg**. The two teams are part of Group A.

India will face Afghanistan on **October 14 in Tshwane** in their third Group Stage match.

### Zimbabwe vs India – October 24

India will play their final Group Stage match against Zimbabwe on **October 24 at Victoria Falls**.

## ODI World Cup 2027 Tournament Format

The 2027 edition features a three-stage structure before the final.

First, three teams will compete in the Super Series in Namibia, with the winner joining the Group Stage.

The 12 Group Stage teams will then be divided into two groups of six. The top three teams from each group will qualify for the Super 7 phase, while the better fourth-placed team will take the seventh Super 7 position.

The seven Super 7 teams will play six matches each. The top four teams will then advance to the semi-finals.
