Hardik Pandya's move out of Mumbai Indians now looks increasingly likely. Reports from [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/cricket/ipl-2027-trade-talks-hardik-pandya-mi-pbks-arshdeep-singh-10949809/lite/) and [Cricbuzz](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-news/140825/hardik-arshdeep-trade-nears-completion-formalities-remain), along with other outlets, indicate that Mumbai Indians and Punjab Kings have reached an agreement in principle, with the remaining formalities and approvals still to be completed.

The trade news broke on a match day, which is why coverage came a little later. Here's why three other franchises' deals reportedly fell through, and why Punjab Kings emerged as the likely destination.

## Why CSK-Hardik Pandya Trade Fell Through

CSK wanted Hardik, and talks were underway. But Mumbai reportedly demanded an overseas player in return, with Nathan Ellis mentioned in the trade discussions. CSK was not willing to part with him, so the deal collapsed.

Yes, CSK's pace attack leans on overseas bowlers and has injury worries. But, come on! You are choosing Nathan Ellis over Hardik Pandya, one of the best white-ball all-rounders in the world? Excuse me?! Any other team would have handed over an Ellis-type player in a heartbeat and thanked Mumbai for taking him. CSK had the deal in their hands and let it slip away. This looks like a total blunder.

## Why KKR-Hardik Pandya Trade Fell Through

KKR and Mumbai held extended talks. Mumbai reportedly asked for Harshit Rana plus a batter, most likely Angkrish Raghuvanshi. KKR treated the players as difficult to part with, and the deal ended there.

And this is where it hurts. KKR has finished in the bottom three, and you still said no? Excuse me?! This is the same team that let Shubman Gill, Shreyas Iyer, Shivam Mavi, and Kamlesh Nagarkoti walk away, and now you are suddenly attached to one young uncapped player? A captain-level superstar was sitting right there, and you could not even pull the trigger. KKR will regret this.

Some fans are calling it a "small-club mentality," and honestly, it is hard to argue. This deal came and went out of your hands, and all you did was spectate while Punjab moved ahead.

## Why GT-Hardik Trade Deal Fell Through

![Hardik Pandya in a Gujarat Titans jersey with the headline GT-Hardik trade possible](/images/Hardik%20Pandya%20GT%20Trade%20Possible_.png)

Gujarat Titans were reportedly Hardik's first preference after leaving Mumbai. He initially wanted to return as captain. GT declined, so he offered to come back as a player only. Talks continued, but Mumbai reportedly asked for Kagiso Rabada and GT refused.

GT's pace attack, with Rabada and Siraj, was among the best in the powerplay and death overs last season, which explains their reluctance.

## The Punjab Kings Hardik Pandya Deal

Cricbuzz and multiple reports now indicate that Hardik is set to join Punjab Kings, with the deal awaiting the remaining player formalities and approval from the IPL authorities. The latest reports indicate a direct one-for-one swap:

- **Arshdeep Singh**, the left-arm pacer, moves to Mumbai Indians.
- **Hardik Pandya** moves to Punjab Kings.

An earlier version of the proposed deal reportedly included wicketkeeper-batter Vishnu Vinod, but the latest reports on November 7 indicate that no third player will be involved.

Interestingly, Mumbai approached several franchises while exploring a trade for Hardik. Arshdeep was a key target because Mumbai wanted an Indian left-arm fast bowler who could complement Jasprit Bumrah.

Cricbuzz has also reported discussions around a possible Punjab Kings-Lucknow Super Giants swap, with Marcus Stoinis potentially moving in exchange for Mohsin Khan.

The Hardik-Arshdeep trade is expected to be formally confirmed once the remaining approvals and player formalities are completed.

### Who Wins the MI-PBKS Trade?

**Mumbai Indians look like clear winners.**

- Arshdeep Singh and Jasprit Bumrah could form one of the strongest T20 pace pairs in the world. Both have been part of back-to-back World Cup-winning squads.
- Both had a poor last season, but together their roles complement each other. One contains, while the other takes wickets.
- Mumbai's death bowling could be sorted, and they free up an overseas slot.
- Arshdeep also gives Mumbai a long-term Indian pace option alongside Bumrah.

**Punjab Kings took a big gamble.**

- On cricketing merit, Punjab arguably did not need Hardik. They already have fast-bowling all-rounders like Marcus Stoinis, Azmatullah Omarzai, and Marco Jansen.
- Losing a proven match-winner like Arshdeep in his prime is a real cost.
- But the deal makes sense beyond the field. Hardik adds brand value and star power, and Punjab's fan base has been growing steadily.
- Captaincy is not on offer since Shreyas Iyer leads the side, so Hardik is expected to join as a player.
- Punjab could free up money by releasing players and improve their bowling elsewhere.
- If it works, Punjab could move to another level. If it doesn't, the team's balance suffers. Either way, taking that risk deserves respect.

## Rajasthan Royals Offer in the Hardik Pandya Deal

Rajasthan Royals were also part of the discussions, with reports suggesting that they could offer Hardik the captaincy and were willing to discuss **Yashasvi Jaiswal and Ravi Bishnoi**.

Mumbai reportedly did not accept because they wanted a front-line fast bowler in the deal, which RR could not provide, and RR did not want to trade Jofra Archer at that time.

Many believe Mumbai may have made a mistake here. Jaiswal is a Mumbai-born batter who could be Rohit Sharma's successor, and Bishnoi would suit Wankhede's conditions. Both would have been guaranteed starters.

On the other hand, Mumbai's pace department needed fixing, especially with Deepak Chahar's uncertain form and the possible release of Trent Boult. Their negotiating approach was sharp: they explored Hardik's availability with several teams, evaluated the offers, and eventually moved ahead with Punjab's Arshdeep proposal.

## Final Thoughts

Mumbai Indians, Punjab Kings, and Rajasthan Royals all showed ambition in this negotiation, discussing stars like Jaiswal and Arshdeep. Meanwhile, KKR could not reach an agreement over the players Mumbai wanted, while CSK's overseas-player constraints reportedly complicated their approach.

The Hardik-Arshdeep swap now appears close, but it should be described as awaiting formalities rather than officially completed until the IPL authorities confirm it. More trade news could follow before the retention deadline.
