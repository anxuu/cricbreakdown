India defeated New Zealand by 22 runs in the fifth and final ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on November 15, 2026. Defending a total of 209, India's bowlers dismissed the hosts for 187 in 48.1 overs to seal the five-match series 3-1.

India were bowled out in 42.1 overs after New Zealand's bowlers made regular breakthroughs. Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan top-scored with 46 runs each, while Naman Dhir added 22. Mohammed Siraj and Arshdeep Singh contributed 28 valuable runs from the final two batting positions.

New Zealand's chase began with a promising partnership, but India's attack kept taking wickets. Arshdeep Singh finished with 3/30, while Mohammed Siraj and Axar Patel claimed two wickets each. Gurnoor Brar, who was named Player of the Series after taking nine wickets across the tour, was not part of the playing XI for this match.

## Arshdeep Singh 3/30: Match-Winning Spell at Bay Oval

Arshdeep Singh delivered the standout bowling performance, taking three wickets for 30 runs from his 10 overs. He removed Will Young, Nick Kelly and Kristian Clarke, disrupting New Zealand's batting at important moments.

Arshdeep also scored 14 runs from just four balls, including two sixes. His late contribution helped India move beyond 200, and his bowling then played a major role in defending that total. He was named Player of the Match.

## Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan Lead India's Batting

Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan both scored 46, providing India's most substantial contributions with the bat. Their efforts helped India recover after early wickets, although New Zealand's bowlers continued to make breakthroughs and prevented a bigger partnership from developing.

The lower-order contribution was also important. Siraj remained unbeaten on 14, and Arshdeep's rapid 14 from four balls helped India reach 209.

## India Defend 210 After New Zealand Reach 64/1

New Zealand began the chase positively and reached 64/1, but India's bowlers changed the course of the innings with regular wickets. Arshdeep led the pace attack, Siraj dismissed Tim Seifert and Finn Allen, and the other bowlers kept the scoring under control.

The decisive moment came when Naman Dhir dismissed Mitchell Santner, who had scored 24. Axar Patel then removed Jacob Duffy and Blair Tickner to finish the innings at 187 and secure India's 22-run win.

## Gurnoor Brar Named Player of the Series With Nine Wickets

Gurnoor Brar was named Player of the Series after taking nine wickets during the five-match ODI series. His performances across the tour made him one of India's leading contributors with the ball, even though he did not play in the final ODI.

Brar's best bowling figures in the series were 4/30, and he finished with an economy rate of 4.87 across four matches.
