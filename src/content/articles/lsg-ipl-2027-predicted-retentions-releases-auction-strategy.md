## LUCKNOW SUPER GIANTS IPL 2027 TRADE UPDATE: RISHABH PANT AND KULDEEP YADAV EXCHANGE

One of the biggest proposed developments ahead of IPL 2027 is the player exchange between Lucknow Super Giants and Delhi Capitals.

The hypothetical trade scenario involves two established Indian international players, with Rishabh Pant moving to Delhi Capitals and Kuldeep Yadav joining Lucknow Super Giants.

### 1. RISHABH PANT MOVES FROM LSG TO DELHI CAPITALS

Rishabh Pant's potential departure from Lucknow Super Giants would be one of the biggest talking points ahead of IPL 2027.

Pant joined LSG for ₹27 crore during the IPL 2025 mega-auction and subsequently led the franchise. Under this proposed trade scenario, he would move to Delhi Capitals.

- Previous Team: Lucknow Super Giants
- Proposed New Team: Delhi Capitals
- Revised Trade Price: ₹15 Crore

Pant's departure would represent a major change for LSG, particularly considering his leadership responsibilities and batting position.

The franchise could now look towards Nicholas Pooran and other senior players to take greater responsibility in the leadership group.

### 2. KULDEEP YADAV JOINS LSG FROM DELHI CAPITALS

In exchange for Rishabh Pant, Lucknow Super Giants are predicted to acquire Indian left-arm wrist-spinner Kuldeep Yadav from Delhi Capitals.

- Previous Team: Delhi Capitals
- Proposed New Team: Lucknow Super Giants
- Contract Price: ₹13.50 Crore

Kuldeep's arrival could initially be viewed as a major boost for LSG's bowling department. His ability to take wickets during the middle overs could provide the franchise with a reliable specialist spinner.

However, despite acquiring him through the proposed trade, LSG could consider releasing Kuldeep ahead of the retention deadline to create additional financial flexibility and explore other squad combinations. This prediction therefore does not include Kuldeep in the retained-player salary calculation.
