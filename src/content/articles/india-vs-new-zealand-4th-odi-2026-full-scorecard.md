Will Young was the leading run-scorer in the India vs New Zealand 4th ODI, producing an outstanding 125 off 108 deliveries.

Young completed his half-century in 49 balls and reached his century in 88 deliveries. The knock was his fifth ODI century and his highest individual score in the format.

Tim Seifert's 58 and Finn Allen's 41 provided valuable support as New Zealand finished with 333 runs.

## Matt Henry 6/27: Six-Wicket Haul Destroys India's Batting Order

Matt Henry delivered a career-best ODI bowling performance, finishing with figures of 6/27.

His opening over changed the match completely as he dismissed Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli and Shreyas Iyer for ducks.

Henry later removed Ishan Kishan, Kuldeep Yadav and Gurnoor Brar to complete his six-wicket haul.

Matthew Fisher also made an important contribution with 3/31, ensuring India could not recover from the early collapse.
