## India vs New Zealand 4th ODI Match Analysis: My POV

### If India Bat First

India will look to establish a strong opening partnership through Rohit Sharma and Ruturaj Gaikwad.

Virat Kohli's experience could provide stability, while Shreyas Iyer and Tilak Varma will be important during the middle overs. Ishan Kishan could provide an aggressive option in the middle order, while Axar Patel and Naman Dhir offer additional batting depth.

If India negotiate the early movement successfully, they could target a total around 270–290 runs.

However, New Zealand's pace attack, particularly Kyle Jamieson and Jacob Duffy, could create problems during the powerplay.

### If New Zealand Bat First

New Zealand will need a strong start through Finn Allen and Will Young.

Nick Kelly and Tim Seifert could play important roles in building the innings, while Dean Foxcroft and Michael Bracewell will have responsibilities during the middle overs.

Mitchell Santner could become a key contributor with both bat and ball.

A total around 260–280 runs could give New Zealand a reasonable opportunity to challenge India.

### If India Bowl First

Arshdeep Singh and Mohammed Siraj could look to exploit early movement with the new ball.

Gurnoor Brar provides another pace-bowling option and could be important if the conditions offer assistance to seamers.

Axar Patel will be valuable during the middle overs, particularly against New Zealand's aggressive batting combination.

India will need to dismiss Finn Allen early and prevent Mitchell Santner and Michael Bracewell from building a substantial partnership.

### If New Zealand Bowl First

Kyle Jamieson and Jacob Duffy could be particularly dangerous if conditions favour swing and seam movement.

Removing Rohit Sharma, Ruturaj Gaikwad and Virat Kohli early could significantly change the direction of the contest.

Mitchell Santner's role will also be important during the middle overs, where he could look to restrict India's scoring rate.

New Zealand will need to maintain pressure throughout the innings rather than relying only on early wickets.

Weather could once again become an important talking point ahead of the fourth ODI. After rain affected the series and completely washed out the third ODI in Hamilton, there will be considerable attention on conditions in Mount Maunganui.

The latest local forecast should be checked closer to match time, as weather conditions can change. Any rain or interruption could affect the number of overs available and the teams' approach to setting or chasing a target.
